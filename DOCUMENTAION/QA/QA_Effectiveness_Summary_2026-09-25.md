## Principles agreed today (for future edits)
- Rate against the **stated goal only**; don't add practice for other skills into a drill.
- Russian cue must point to the **right** answer (no Russian-interference traps in memorisation drills).
- A context may give what's needed to know the cell (e.g. gender), never the answer itself.
- Memorised example sentences are **wanted** — they are anchors in speech. So sentences must be natural, everyday phrases.
- Known items keep coming up (prevents confusion); 10-card rounds (3–4 min) are the intended format.
- Tables: minimal and visual — examples instead of rules, show only what differs.
